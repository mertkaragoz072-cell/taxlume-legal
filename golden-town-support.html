Getting help

Email mertkaragoz072@gmail.com. Please say which device and OS version you are on, and what you were doing when the problem happened — that is usually enough to find it.

I lost my town

Progress is saved on your device only, so uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it, and we have no copy to restore from. Before switching devices, open the Goals screen and export your backup code, then paste that code into the new install.

Does the game cost anything?

No. Golden Town is free, has no ads, no in-app purchases, and no account. Everything happens on your device.

Turning off notifications

The app's reminders are local notifications. You can turn them off in your device's system settings for Golden Town, and the game plays exactly the same without them.
